Both draft lenses returned nothing usable. The `frontend-backend-contract` lens's draft was an unfinished tool-call trace (a single `Grep` invocation with no result or synthesized claim) — there was no actual finding to verify or adjudicate, only an implied hypothesis worth checking by hand: that the frontend's shop/square/tickets/video integration surface calls backend routes that no longer exist. The `cross-repo-dead-code` lens explicitly reported a clean result.

Per this campaign's false-positive defense, an absence claim can't become a finding without a source read in both repos, so that implied hypothesis was checked directly against `routes/api/platforms.php` and `PlatformRegistryServiceProvider.php` rather than accepted or dropped on priors. Every endpoint the reviewed frontend files call was traced to a live backend route with a matching request/response contract:

- `GET/POST /platforms/shop/brands`, `PATCH/DELETE /platforms/shop/brands/{id}`, `GET /platforms/shop/brands/{id}/products`, `PUT /platforms/shop/brands/{id}/selection`, `GET/PATCH /platforms/shop/settings` — all present in `ShopController`, and `AddShopBrandRequest`/`ShopController::addBrand` does accept the `storeApi` client-probe fallback `shop-brand-modals.tsx`'s `postAddBrand` depends on.
- `POST/GET/DELETE /platforms/square/*` — present in `SquareController`.
- `POST/GET/DELETE /platforms/{eventbrite,humanitix}/{connect,accounts,events,selection}` and the unified `/platforms/events/{add,selection,custom/{id}}` facade — present in `EventsController`/`EventbriteController`/`HumanitixController`.
- `youtube`, `vimeo`, `youtube-music` are registered with `PlatformRouteShape::MultiAccount` and the highlights flag set (`true`), which is what emits the `/accounts`, `/recent`, `/highlights` routes `youtube-section.tsx` and `use-real-refresh.ts` call; the six link-only socials (`x`, `linkedin`, `threads`, `reddit`, `tiktok`, `facebook`) are registered with `PlatformRouteShape::LinkOnly`, matching `socials-section.tsx`'s plain connect/selection usage.
- `linkMode`'s wire values (`"product" | "checkout"`) match `Site::SHOP_LINK_MODES` / `UpdateShopSettingsRequest`'s `Rule::in(...)` exactly, and `autoLatest` is a plain boolean on both sides.

No route, capability, or shape mismatch surfaced. Within the given frontend file set, every exported symbol (`formatPrice`, `PLATFORM_LABELS`, `eventSubtitle`, `UnifiedEvent`/`UnifiedAccount`/`EventsSelection`, `eventsSelectionQuery`, `videoPickerConfig`, `VIDEO_CONFIGS`, etc.) is consumed by a sibling file, a co-located test, or is annotated with a named external consumer (route-mounted sub-pages this bundle didn't include) — none is provably dead.

One adjacent observation was surfaced during verification but is out of scope for this bundle and is not reported as a finding: the shop connect routes don't carry the `platform.available` HTTP-edge gate that square/fresha/instagram/eventbrite/humanitix connect routes do (`EnsurePlatformAvailable`'s docblock says it exists to 503 "before any scrape"). The docblock also states a persistence-layer backstop (`ManagesIntegrationConnection`) blocks the DB write "for every platform/verb" regardless, so this is at most a same-repo defense-in-depth/ordering nuance — not a cross-repo contract mismatch or frontend dead code, so it doesn't fit either XREPO or XDEAD and was left for a security/hardening-lens pass instead of forced into this bundle.
